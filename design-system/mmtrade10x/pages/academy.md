# Academy and challenge override

- Covers academy, academy design, progress, challenges, organizers and challenge detail.
- Course cards show verified title, lesson count, completion and next lesson.
- Progress uses a clear stepper/progress bar with text percentage and current stage.
- Lesson detail prioritizes reading width, video aspect ratio, captions/transcript and next/previous actions.
- Do not use decorative charts for learning progress; preserve course access and challenge eligibility rules.
